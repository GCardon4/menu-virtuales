## Brand & Style

The brand personality is energetic, flavorful, unapologetic, and deeply rooted in Colombian street-food culture. It pairs the warmth and camaraderie of neighborhood dining with a modern, crave-inducing digital presentation. Targeted at diners accessing the menu instantly via QR code on mobile devices in varied restaurant ambient lighting (from warm Edison bulbs to low patio evenings), the interface evokes immediate appetite, speed, and approachability.

The visual style blends **Tactile Modern Street-Gourmet** with **High-Contrast Dark Mode**. Rather than flat, sterile corporate cards, the UI leverages deep charcoal and textured rustic brick undertones, luminous golden mustard accents (reminiscent of melted cheddar and crispy papitas), and vibrant fiery salsa reds. Elements feel physical and punchy: price tags mimic street-stand badges, category chips act like magnetic counters, and item imagery sits front and center with rich, saturated photography.

## Colors

The color architecture is calibrated specifically for handheld viewing in low-to-medium ambient light while maximizing food desirability:

- **Primary (`#F59E0B`):** Crisp mustard gold. Used for primary CTA badges, selected category indicators, active order tallies, and highlighted ingredients.
- **Secondary (`#E11D48`):** Sizzling tomato-chili red. Used for promotional flags, combo callouts, spicy tags, and high-energy interactive touchpoints.
- **Tertiary (`#FBBF24`):** Warm molten butter yellow. Serves as hover/focus glows, secondary price accents, and star ratings.
- **Neutral Core (`#181514` to `#241E1C`):** Charred-brick black-brown foundation. Provides a rich backdrop that allows vivid food photography and golden typography to pop without causing harsh eye strain.
- **Surface Elevation:** 
  - Canvas: `#14100F`
  - Card Surfaces: `#211B19`
  - Elevated Popovers/Modals: `#2D2522`
  - Subtle Borders: `#3B302C`
- **Text & Contrast:** 
  - Primary text: `#FFFDF7` (Warm Cream) for maximum contrast ratio (>13:1).
  - Secondary text: `#D4C3B7` (Toasted Sesame) for descriptions and ingredients.
  - Tertiary / Subtext: `#9C8E84` for notes and meta captions.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Epilogue** for display headlines, menu titles, and COP prices with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for clear, legible ingredient lists and UI labels.

- **Epilogue** brings chunky, geometric confidence with a street-urban edge. Its heavy weights (`700` and `800`) deliver high visibility for colloquial item titles (e.g., *"Gordita pero Sabrosa"*, *"Avíspese Pues"*) and unmistakable currency notation (COP values like `$13.000` or `$26.900`).
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides open counters, friendly terminals, and high legibility at dense mobile sizes (12px–14px), essential for reading long ingredient descriptions (*papas a la francesa, carne desmechada, tocineta, maicitos, ripio de papa, guacamole*) on dimly lit mobile screens.
- Currency prices must always be rendered with tabular figures and strong weight (`price-badge-*`) to allow instant cognitive scanning when comparing portion sizes (Mini vs. Combo).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for **mobile-first fluid execution**, optimized for one-handed thumb interaction on mobile viewports ranging from 360px to 430px wide, scaling comfortably onto tablets.

- **Base Grid Rhythm:** 4-column fluid system on mobile with 12px (`gutter-mobile`) gutters and 16px (`margin-mobile`) horizontal canvas margin. Tablet breakpoints (768px+) expand to 8 columns with 16px gutters and 24px margins.
- **Vertical Flow:** Touch targets strictly adhere to a minimum 48px height for single-tap ease while eating. Card gaps maintain 12px to 16px vertical distance to distinguish item cards clearly without excessive scrolling.
- **Sticky Sticky Header & Category Ribbon:** A compact 56px sticky top header containing restaurant brand mark, table number pill, and search icon, followed immediately by a horizontal sticky category carousel with edge-fade masking.
- **Safe Area Bottom Offsets:** Mobile screens allocate a persistent 72px bottom padding buffer to prevent floating action buttons (e.g., "Ver Pedido / Llamar Mesero") from obscuring the last menu item.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal container layering** blended with **warm ambient lighting glows** to recreate the ambiance of neon signs against exposed rustic brickwork:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Dark raw tone (`#14100F`) featuring an optional subtle CSS noise or dark brick raster grain at 6% opacity.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Surfaces):** Soft elevated charcoal `#211B19` framed with a hairline perimeter border (`1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`). This eliminates dark-on-dark muddying and renders individual food items distinct.
- **Level 2 (Sticky Headers & Modals):** Semi-opaque background (`#1C1614` at 92% opacity) treated with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` to maintain context while scrolling through dishes.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Badges & Sticky CTAs):** Accent surfaces featuring warm bottom-drop shadows: `box-shadow: 0 4px 18px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.22), 0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
- **Level 4 (Promo & Combo Badges):** High-energy fiery drops: `box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.28)`.

## Shapes

This design system uses a balanced **level 2 roundedness** (8px base radius, 16px on large containers), paired with full-pill elements for interactive tags:

- **Buttons & Price Pills:** Full pill (`border-radius: 9999px`) provides a friendly, approachable touchpoint that feels like a physical diner chip or bottle cap.
- **Menu Item Cards:** `rounded-lg` (16px / 1rem) creates smooth, modern container bounds that gently hug top food photography and bottom pricing rows.
- **Product Image Thumbnails:** `rounded-md` (10px) to soft-edge food photos while preserving screen real estate.
- **Category Chips:** Full pill (`9999px`) for seamless sideways swiping and thumb tapping.
- **Bottom Sheets / Detail Drawer:** `24px` rounded top corners (`rounded-t-2xl`) for natural, upward-pulling tactile gestures.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Agregar al Pedido", "Ver Combo"):** Pill-shaped, gradient background from `#F59E0B` to `#EA580C`, dark charcoal bold typography (`#181514`), 48px height, 16px horizontal padding. Active state subtly scales down (`transform: scale(0.97)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#F59E0B`, text in `#FFFDF7`.
- **Icon Quick-Add Button (`+`):** 36x36px circular button nested at the bottom-right of menu cards with warm gold fill and high-contrast dark plus symbol.

### Category Chips (Filtros de Menú)
- Horizontal swipeable rail with `no-scrollbar`.
- **Inactive:** `#27201E` background, `#D4C3B7` text, subtle border (`1px solid #3B302C`).
- **Active / Selected:** Vivid mustard `#F59E0B` background, bold `#181514` text, soft golden shadow glow. Accompanied by micro category icons (fries, burger, drink icons).

### Food Item Cards (Tarjetas de Producto)
- **Layout:** Two variants:
  1. *Featured / Hero Card (Papas Pues specials):* Full-width image on top (140px height with dark gradient overlay at bottom), item name in `headline-md` Cream, ingredient description in `body-sm` Sesame, dual price badge (e.g., "Mini $13.000" / "Combo $22.900") at footer.
  2. *Compact List Card (Bebidas, Adiciones):* Horizontal split layout: 84x84px rounded image thumbnail on left, title and description center, bold price badge and `+` action button pinned to the right.

### Price Badges
- High-contrast visual units. Solid dark gold background (`#F59E0B`), dark text (`#14100F`), or subtle dark pill with border and gold text. COP currency symbol formatted compactly (`$13.000 COP`).

### Modifiers & Adiciones (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Custom rounded radio buttons and checkboxes (22x22px).
- **Checked state:** Primary gold fill with dark checkmark.
- Item row provides clear inline delta pricing (`+ $4.000` or `+ $6.000`) aligned flush right in bold tertiary color.

### Search & Quick Filter Input
- Full-width pill input with deep surface fill (`#211B19`), warm gold search magnifying glass icon on the left, `#FFFDF7` typed input, and `#9C8E84` placeholder text: *"Busca tu antojo (ej. tocineta, calzone)..."*.

### Table Indicator Badge (Mesa QR)
- Pinned at top bar: Pill badge displaying table info (e.g., *"Mesa 04"*), styled in dark crimson `#E11D48` background with white bold lettering, reassuring customers their location is registered.
